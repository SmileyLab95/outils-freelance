Special cases

Sales of £250 or less including VAT

A VAT-registered business can issue a simplified invoice for these sales: your name and address, your VAT registration number, the time of supply, a description of the goods or services, and for each VAT rate the total including VAT and the rate. Exempt supplies can't be on a simplified invoice, and your customer can still ask for a full one. In Northern Ireland, simplified invoices can't be used for goods sold to customers in the EU.

Limited companies

Your full registered company name is required on invoices. Your registered number, registered office address and where you're registered (England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland) are required on business letters, order forms and your website, so adding them to your invoices is good practice.

Frequently asked questions

What must a UK invoice include?
A unique invoice number, your business name, address and contact details, your customer's name and address, a clear description of what you're charging for, the date of the goods or service, the invoice date, the amounts charged, any VAT, and the total owed. Sole traders and limited companies add their own details, listed in the checklist.
What extra details does a VAT invoice need?
Your VAT registration number, a sequential invoice number, the time of supply (tax point), and for each item the quantity, unit price, VAT rate and amount excluding VAT; then the rate of any cash discount, the total excluding VAT, the total VAT in sterling, and the reverse charge, zero-rate or exempt wording where it applies. For a sale of £250 or less including VAT, a simplified invoice is enough, unless your customer asks for a full one.
I'm a limited company: what do I need to show?
Your full registered company name, exactly as on your certificate of incorporation. If a director's name appears other than in the body text or as the signatory, show the names of all directors. Your registered number, registered office address and where you're registered must appear on business letters, order forms and your website; adding them to invoices is good practice.
I'm not VAT-registered: what changes?
Leave VAT out completely: a business that isn't VAT-registered must not show VAT on its invoices.
When should I send a VAT invoice?
Normally within 30 days of the date of supply, to VAT-registered customers when the sale is standard- or reduced-rated.
