What a good NDA covers

  • The purpose: the information may be used for this purpose only, so describe it precisely.
  • What is confidential, and what is not: public information, what you already knew, what a third party gave you freely and what you developed on your own are excluded.
  • How long it lasts: a disclosure period, then a number of years of confidentiality.
  • The end of the discussions: the information is returned or destroyed, and nobody is obliged to sign a contract.

Sign the agreement before sharing anything, mark your documents “Confidential” and keep a record of what you sent and when. When the project goes ahead, write the freelance contract.

Frequently asked questions

When should a freelancer sign an NDA?
Before any confidential information changes hands: when a client wants to share plans, code, data or customer lists before hiring you, or when you pitch your own idea. If you plan to patent an invention, GOV.UK warns that you may lose the right to a patent if it becomes public before you apply, and that you may need an NDA to discuss it with anyone apart from a patent attorney.
Mutual or one-way NDA?
The Intellectual Property Office advises a one-way NDA if only one side discloses information and a mutual NDA if both do, without turning a one-way agreement into an artificial mutual one. It also advises stating the permitted purpose as precisely as possible.
Why is my one-way NDA worded as a deed?
The Intellectual Property Office notes that a one-way NDA may need to be executed as a deed to be enforceable. Under the law of England and Wales or Northern Ireland, the generator words a one-way agreement as a deed: each party signs in the presence of a witness, who signs and adds their name and address, and a company can sign through a director in front of a witness. Scotland has its own rules, so a one-way agreement under Scots law keeps ordinary signatures.
Can an NDA stop someone reporting a crime?
Not always. Since 1 October 2025, in England and Wales, a term in an agreement made from that date is void to the extent it stops a victim of crime from making certain disclosures, for example to the police or a qualified lawyer (Victims and Prisoners Act 2024, section 17). The template also says that nothing in it prevents a disclosure the law protects or permits.
Is this template legal advice?
No. It covers common business-to-business discussions. For a high-value secret, an invention you plan to patent, an agreement under foreign law or terms the other side wants to negotiate, have a lawyer review it.
