    How the self-employed tax year works

    • The tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April. You file your return and pay the balance by the following 31 January.
    • Payments on account spread next year's bill over 31 January and 31 July, so your first year often feels expensive: you pay last year's tax plus half of this year's.
    • Making Tax Digital adds four quarterly updates from compatible software, and a final declaration that replaces the tax return. See who must join and when.
    • VAT returns follow your own VAT quarters. Check your prices with the VAT calculator.

    For information only. Your VAT periods and any HMRC notices in your online account take precedence.

    Frequently asked questions

    When is the Self Assessment deadline?
    31 January 2027 for the 2025/26 tax year if you file online, and 31 October 2026 on paper. The tax you still owe is also due on 31 January, with your first payment on account for 2026/27 if you have to make one.
    What are payments on account?
    Two advance payments towards next year's bill, each half of last year's tax, due on 31 January and 31 July. You make them if your last Self Assessment bill was over £1,000, unless at least 80% of your tax was already deducted at source. Estimate them with the self-employed tax calculator.
    When are Making Tax Digital quarterly updates due?
    By 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May, for the quarters ending 5 July, 5 October, 5 January and 5 April. Sole traders and landlords with qualifying income over £50,000 joined in April 2026; those over £30,000 join on 6 April 2027. Check whether you're in with our Making Tax Digital guide.
    When are VAT returns due?
    One calendar month and seven days after the end of each VAT period: for a quarter ending 31 March, by 7 May. If you pay by Direct Debit, HMRC collects the payment three working days after that.
    How do I add these dates to my calendar?
    Click “Add to my calendar (.ics)”: the file contains the upcoming deadlines that match your settings, with a reminder 3 days before each one. Open it with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
