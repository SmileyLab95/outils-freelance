What every freelance contract should cover

  • Scope and deliverables: the more precise, the fewer arguments about what's included.
  • Fees, VAT, deposit and payment terms: a deposit protects your cash flow, and the Late Payment Act gives you interest and compensation. Chase late invoices with a payment reminder letter.
  • Status: a contract for services, with control and substitution, reflects that you're self-employed.
  • Ownership: assigning the IP only once you're paid in full is your best protection against non-payment.
  • Termination: notice, and payment for the work already done.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a written contract as a freelancer?
It isn't required by law, but it's the best way to agree scope, payment and ownership before problems arise. It also documents that you work as a self-employed contractor, which matters for employment status and, if you work through a limited company, for IR35.
Does a contract decide my IR35 status?
No. HMRC and the courts look at how the work is really done: control, a genuine right of substitution and whether there's an obligation to offer and accept work. The contract should reflect reality. If you work through your own limited company for a medium or large client, the client decides your status and must give you a status determination statement.
Who owns the work: me or my client?
As a contractor, you own the copyright in what you create unless you assign it in writing. This template assigns it once you're paid in full, which also gives you leverage if a client doesn't pay.
Can I charge interest on late payments?
Yes. Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, you can claim 8% a year above the Bank of England base rate, plus fixed compensation of £40 to £100 per invoice. Work it out with the late payment interest calculator.
Is this contract legally binding?
Yes, once both parties sign it, including with an electronic signature. It is a general template for business-to-business projects, not legal advice: for a large project, work abroad or sensitive data, have a lawyer review it.
