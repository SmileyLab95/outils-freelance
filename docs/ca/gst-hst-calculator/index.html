    GST/HST for freelancers

    • Small supplier: you don't have to register while your taxable supplies don't go over $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters.
    • Charge the client's province: for most services, the rate depends on where your client is, not where you are.
    • On your invoices: once registered, show your GST/HST number (and your QST number in Québec) and the tax amounts. Our invoice generator adds the right lines for each province.

    Information only. Check the CRA, Revenu Québec or your provincial tax site for your specific goods or services.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are the sales tax rates in Canada in 2026?
    GST is 5% everywhere. Ontario charges 13% HST; New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Prince Edward Island 15% HST; Nova Scotia 14% HST since April 1, 2025. Québec adds 9.975% QST, British Columbia 7% PST, Saskatchewan 6% PST and Manitoba 7% RST to the GST. Alberta and the territories charge GST only.
    How do I calculate HST from a total?
    Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. In Ontario: total ÷ 1.13 gives the price before tax, and the difference is the HST. For example, $113 ÷ 1.13 = $100, so the HST is $13.
    Do I have to charge GST/HST as a freelancer?
    Only once you are registered. Registration is mandatory when your taxable revenue goes over $30,000 in a single quarter or over four consecutive quarters (the small supplier threshold). You can register voluntarily below it to claim input tax credits.
    Which province's rate do I charge?
    For most services sold to Canadian clients, the rate of the province where your client is located (place of supply), not where you live. Services to clients outside Canada are generally zero-rated.
    Do freelancers charge PST?
    In British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba, PST or RST applies to goods and to a list of specific services, but most professional services such as consulting, design, writing or coaching are not taxable. Check your province's list for your service.
