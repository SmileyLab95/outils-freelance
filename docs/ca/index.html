Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?
Yes. Every tool is free, with no account, no email and no usage limit.
Do the tools work for every province?
Yes. The tax and sales tax calculators cover all ten provinces and three territories, including Québec's QPP, QPIP and QST.
Is my data safe?
It never leaves your device: calculations run in your browser, and what you type is only remembered locally to save you time next visit.
