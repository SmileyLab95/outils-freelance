Frequently asked questions

What must a Canadian invoice include?
Your business name, the invoice date, a unique invoice number, your client's name, a description of the work and the total. Once you are registered for GST/HST, add your registration number (9-digit business number + RT0001) and the tax charged, so your client can claim input tax credits: for invoices of $100 or more, the CRA expects your GST/HST number and the tax amount or rate; from $500, also the client's name and the terms of payment.
Which GST/HST rate do I put on the invoice?
Generally the rate of the province where your client is located: 13% HST in Ontario, 15% in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and PEI, 14% in Nova Scotia, and 5% GST elsewhere, plus 9.975% QST for Québec clients if you are registered with Revenu Québec.
Do I charge GST/HST if I'm a small supplier?
No. If your taxable revenue is $30,000 or less over four consecutive quarters and you haven't registered voluntarily, you don't charge GST/HST. Choose “Not registered” in the form.
How do I save the invoice as a PDF?
