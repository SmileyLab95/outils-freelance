    How to get paid faster

    • Invoice right away, the day the work is delivered or the milestone is reached.
    • Use short terms: Net 15 or due on receipt for small clients, Net 30 at most for companies.
    • Make paying easy: include a payment link or your ACH details on every invoice.
    • Ask for a deposit on estimates (30 to 50%) before you start.
    • State your late fee in your contract and on the invoice, and follow up with our payment reminder generator.

    New client asking for your Social Security number? Send a completed Form W-9 instead. Clients who pay you $2,000 or more in 2026 may send you a Form 1099-NEC early in 2027.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this invoice generator really free?
    Yes. No account, no watermark, no limit. Create as many invoices and estimates as you need and download them as PDF.
    What should a freelance invoice include?
    Your name or business name and contact details, the client's name and address, a unique invoice number, the invoice date, the due date or payment terms (for example Net 30), a description of the work with quantities and rates, the total, and how to pay you. Adding your late fee terms helps you get paid on time.
    What does Net 30 mean on an invoice?
    The client has 30 days from the invoice date to pay. Net 15 means 15 days; “due on receipt” means immediately. Shorter terms usually mean faster payment, so pick the shortest your clients will accept.
    Do I need to charge sales tax as a freelancer?
    Most states do not tax professional services, but some do, and digital products are often taxable. Check your state's rules; if you need to charge it, enter the rate and the tool adds it to the invoice.
    How do I save the invoice as a PDF?
    Click “Download PDF”, then choose “Save as PDF” as the printer in the window that opens. Your details are remembered on this device so your next invoice takes seconds.
