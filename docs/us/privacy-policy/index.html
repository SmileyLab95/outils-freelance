    Privacy policy

    In short: your data stays with you.

    What you enter stays with you

    The tools on this site run in your browser. The information you enter (your business details, clients, amounts, income) is not sent to any server and is not collected by the publisher.

    Domain name search

    The domain name search is the one tool that sends something out: when you click “Search”, your browser sends the names you are checking straight to the official registries of the extensions (for example Verisign for .com), without cookies or the address of this site. These names do not go through our servers and are not stored by the site. As with any website you visit, each registry sees your connection's IP address and applies its own privacy policy.

    Stored on your device only

    To save you time, some fields are remembered in your browser's local storage, on your device only. You can erase them at any time with the “Clear everything” button of the invoice generator, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

    Cookies and analytics

    We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If this changes, this page will be updated and your consent will be requested where the law requires it.

    Hosting

    Like any web host, GitHub Pages may log technical connection data (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
