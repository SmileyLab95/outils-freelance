The deadlines that matter for freelancers

  • Quarterly estimated taxes cover income tax and self-employment tax on what you earn. Work out the amounts with the quarterly tax calculator.
  • April 15 is the day to file your return (Schedule C and Schedule SE) and pay the balance. See what you'll owe with the self-employment tax calculator.
  • Forms 1099: you send a 1099-NEC to contractors you paid $2,000 or more, and you receive 1099-NEC and 1099-K forms from your clients and payment platforms.

Federal deadlines only. Most states with an income tax use the same estimated tax dates, but check your state's tax agency. For information only.

Frequently asked questions

When are quarterly estimated taxes due?
For 2026 income: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, then January 15, 2027. For 2027 income: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2027, then January 18, 2028 (January 15 is a Saturday and January 17 is Martin Luther King Jr. Day). The periods are uneven: the second one covers only April and May.
Do I have to make estimated tax payments?
Generally yes, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more when you file, after withholding and credits. You avoid the underpayment penalty by paying at least 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's (110% if your AGI was over $150,000). Our quarterly tax calculator works out the amounts.
When do I send 1099-NEC forms?
By February 1, 2027 for payments made in 2026 (January 31 is a Sunday), to each contractor you paid $2,000 or more and to the IRS. The threshold was $600 for payments made in 2025.
Does an extension give me more time to pay?
No. Form 4868 extends the time to file to October 15, 2027, but any tax owed is still due on April 15, 2027. Interest and late-payment penalties run from April 15.
How do I add these dates to my calendar?
Click “Add to my calendar (.ics)”: the file contains the upcoming deadlines that match your settings, with a reminder 3 days before each one. Open it with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, or add a single deadline with its “+ Google Calendar” link.
