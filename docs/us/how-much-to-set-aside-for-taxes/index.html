    A simple system that works

    1. Open a separate savings account just for taxes.
    2. Every time a client pays you, move your percentage to it the same day. Don't wait for the end of the month.
    3. Pay your quarterly estimated taxes from that account: the quarterly tax calculator gives the minimum to avoid a penalty, and the tax calendar reminds you of each due date.
    4. Recalculate every few months as your income changes: the percentage rises with your profit.

    Anything left in the account after you file in April is a bonus, or a head start on next year.

    Frequently asked questions

    What percentage should a freelancer set aside for taxes?
    It depends on your profit, filing status and state. With the 15.3% self-employment tax plus federal income tax, a single filer with no state income tax needs about 17.3% of a $30,000 profit, 19.5% of $50,000 and 22.4% of $100,000. The calculator works out your own percentage from the 2026 IRS rules.
    Should I set aside a percentage of revenue or of profit?
    Of every payment you receive (revenue) is easier to apply. The calculator divides your total tax by your revenue, so expenses are already taken into account: just move that share of each payment to a separate savings account the day it arrives.
    Where should I keep the money?
    In a separate savings account, ideally a high-yield one, that you don't touch for anything else. Pay your quarterly estimated taxes from it on April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.
    What if I also have a job?
    Enter your W-2 wages: they push your business income into higher brackets and count toward the Social Security wage base. If your employer withholds enough, you may need to set aside less, or you can raise your W-4 withholding instead of making quarterly payments.
    Is this the exact amount I'll owe?
    It's an estimate for planning. It uses the 2026 brackets, standard deduction, self-employment tax and QBI deduction, but not credits, retirement contributions, the self-employed health insurance deduction or local taxes. For the detailed breakdown, use the self-employment tax calculator.
