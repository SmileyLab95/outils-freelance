    EI for the self-employed: what you can get

    • Special benefits only: sickness, injury or quarantine (up to 26 weeks), maternity (15 weeks), parental (35 weeks at 55% or 61 weeks at 33%), compassionate care (26 weeks), and care for a critically ill child (35 weeks) or adult (15 weeks). The Employment Insurance Act doesn't give the self-employed benefits when work dries up.
    • How much: 55% of your net self-employment income for the year before your claim, divided by 52 and rounded to the nearest dollar, on income up to $68,900 in 2026: at most $729 a week. Extended parental benefits pay 33%.
    • Waiting period: the first week of your claim isn't paid.

    Signing up: the agreement and the premium

    • Who: self-employed Canadian citizens and permanent residents, by entering into an agreement with the Canada Employment Insurance Commission.
    • When you can claim: once 12 months have passed since you signed up, if your self-employment earnings last year were at least $6,000, indexed each year since 2012 (Service Canada publishes the amount).
    • Premium: 1.63% of your self-employment earnings in 2026 (1.30% in Quebec), on up to $68,900 of earnings, paid with your income tax return every year, including the year you sign up and the year it ends.
    • Leaving: you can end the agreement by written notice as long as you haven't received benefits; it ends on December 31 of that year. Within 60 days of signing up, it's as if you'd never signed up. After receiving benefits, only in the cases set by regulation.

    In Quebec: the QPIP

    • Mandatory: self-employed workers living in Quebec pay QPIP premiums with their tax return (0.764% of business income in 2026, at most $786.92), unless their work income is under $2,000.
    • Benefits: based on last year's insurable income divided by 52, up to $103,000 a year. Basic plan: maternity 18 weeks at 70%, paternity 5 weeks at 70%, parental 32 weeks (7 at 70%, then 25 at 55%). Special plan: 75% for 15, 3 and 25 weeks. You need at least $2,000 of insurable income.
    • EI in Quebec: maternity and parental benefits come from the QPIP, but EI sickness and caregiver benefits remain open if you sign up.
