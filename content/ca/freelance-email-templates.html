Freelance email templates

The emails every freelancer has to write, ready to use. Copy one, fill in the highlighted fields, send.

1. Cold outreach to a prospect
Subject: Quick idea for [Company] Hi [First name], I noticed [specific detail: a recent launch, their website, a job post] and thought of [one concrete improvement]. I help [type of clients] [measurable result]. For example, [similar client] got [number] in [time frame]. Would a 15-minute call next week be useful? I can share two or three specific ideas, no strings attached. Best, [Your name]
2. Following up on a proposal
Subject: Re: Proposal for [project name] Hi [First name], Have you had a chance to look at the proposal? If anything gives you pause (scope, timeline or budget), let me know: we can often adjust it in a few minutes. To start on [date], I would need your go-ahead by [date]. Thanks, [Your name]
3. Announcing a rate increase
Subject: Update to my rates from [date] Hi [First name], Thank you for another great year of working together. Starting [date, at least 30 days away], my rate for new projects will be [new rate]. This reflects [more experience / added services / rising costs] and lets me keep giving your projects the attention they deserve. Anything you book before [date] stays at the current rate. I'm happy to talk it through if you have any questions. Best, [Your name]
4. Handling scope creep
Subject: Re: [request] Hi [First name], Great idea, I'd be glad to help with [new request]. It falls outside the scope we agreed on, so here are two options: 1. I add it as a change order for [price], delivered by [date]. 2. We swap it for [an item still in scope] at no extra cost. Which one works best for you? Best, [Your name]
5. “Your price is too high”
Hi [First name], Thanks for being upfront about it. To make sure I understand: is the total above your budget, or is it the value of some parts that feels unclear? If it's the budget, we can reduce the scope to fit [their budget], for example by [removing or phasing an item]. I would rather adjust what's included than cut corners on quality. What would work best for you? [Your name]
6. Asking for a testimonial
Subject: A quick favor? Hi [First name], I really enjoyed working on [project] with you. Would you be willing to write two or three sentences about the experience? It helps other clients decide to work with me. A few prompts if useful: what was the challenge, what changed after the project, and would you recommend me? And if you know someone who could use [your service], I'd be grateful for an introduction. Thank you! [Your name]
