A simple system that works

  1. Open a separate savings account just for taxes, and GST/HST if you're registered.
  2. Every time a client pays you, move your share to it the same day. Don't wait for the end of the month.
  3. Pay from that account: your quarterly instalments if the CRA asks for them, then the balance by April 30. The tax calendar lists every date, and the GST/HST calculator adds or removes sales tax from any price.
  4. Recalculate every few months as your income changes: the share rises with your profit.

An estimate for planning, based on the 2026 federal and provincial rates. It doesn't replace advice from an accountant for your situation.
