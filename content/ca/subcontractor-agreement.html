What this agreement covers

  • The work and the main contract: what you hand over, the deliverables and dates, and the requirements of your client contract that you pass on in writing. You stay responsible to your client (in Quebec, Civil Code art. 2101).
  • Payment: a fixed fee or a day or hourly rate, paid within the agreed days of the invoice, whether or not your client has paid you, with late interest stated per year as the Interest Act requires.
  • Ownership: the copyright is assigned to you once you have paid, and moral rights are waived, so that you can pass the rights on to your client.
  • Ending early: if your client ends the main contract, you can end this one and pay for the work done; under Quebec law, articles 2125 to 2129 of the Civil Code apply.

Before you subcontract

  • Check that your client contract allows you to subcontract and whether your client must approve the subcontractor.
  • If the subcontractor will handle personal information for your client, agree the required terms in writing first.
  • In Quebec, a standard-form contract must first be given in French: use the French version.
  • Prepare your own contract with your client with the freelance contract.

What this template does not cover

Construction work, government contracts, staffing agencies, and subcontractors based outside Canada. Whether a non-solicitation clause can be enforced depends on the governing law: have it reviewed by a lawyer (or a notary in Quebec) before you rely on it.
