The rules in short

  • Who qualifies: your workspace is your principal place of business, or you use it only for the business and regularly meet clients, customers or patients there.
  • The business part: the workspace's share of your home's finished area, and the share of time if you also use it personally.
  • The limit: no more than your net business income before these expenses; the rest carries forward to the next year.
  • Capital cost allowance on your home isn't included here: claiming it has consequences when you sell your home.
