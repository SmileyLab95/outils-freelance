Payment terms on Canadian invoices

  • Net terms: Net 30 means 30 days after the invoice date; Net 30 EOM means 30 days after the end of the month of the invoice.
  • In writing: agree the terms in your quote or contract, then repeat them on every invoice, with any late payment interest you agreed.
