The rule: the Bank of Canada rate of the day

For income tax, an amount in another currency is converted into Canadian dollars using the relevant spot rate for the day on which the amount arose: the rate quoted by the Bank of Canada on that day or, if none was quoted that day, on the closest preceding day with a rate. Another rate of exchange can be used only if it is acceptable to the Minister (Income Tax Act, section 261).

A client abroad raises other questions (GST/HST, place of supply, withholding): see invoicing clients outside Canada.

Where the rate comes from

When you click “Get the Bank of Canada rate”, your browser asks the Bank of Canada's Valet service directly, without going through our servers; the amount is never sent (privacy). If the service doesn't answer, enter the rate from the Bank of Canada's daily exchange rates.
