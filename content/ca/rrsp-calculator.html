RRSP tips for freelancers

  • Contribute as you earn: a monthly contribution is easier than a lump sum in February.
  • Lower your instalments: an RRSP deduction reduces the tax you owe, so it can reduce your quarterly instalments too. See your numbers with the self-employed tax calculator.
  • Mark the deadline: the tax calendar includes the March 1, 2027 RRSP deadline.
