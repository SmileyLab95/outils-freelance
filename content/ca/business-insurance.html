    Workers' compensation boards

    Each province and territory has its own board. As a self-employed person working alone, you can usually choose personal coverage; as an employer, check whether your industry must be covered.

    Working from home

    Home insurance generally won't cover claims related to a home-based business, and it may cover some business equipment only while it's in your home. Tell your insurer, and review what your policy covers.
