What every freelance contract should cover

  • Scope and deliverables: the more precise, the fewer arguments about what's included.
  • Fees, taxes, deposit and payment terms: say that GST/HST is extra, ask for a deposit and state a yearly interest rate for late payments. Chase late invoices with a payment reminder.
  • Ownership: assign the copyright, and waive moral rights, only once you're paid in full.
  • Termination: notice, and payment for the work already done.
