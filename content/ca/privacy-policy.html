Privacy policy

In short: your data stays with you.

What you enter stays with you

The tools on this site run in your browser. The information you enter (your business details, clients, amounts, income) is not sent to any of our servers and is not collected by the publisher.

Domain name search

The domain name search sends something out: when you click “Search”, your browser sends the names you are checking straight to the official registries of the extensions (for example Verisign for .com), without cookies or the address of the page you are on (only the site's name goes with the request, as with any request from one site to another). These names do not go through our servers and are not stored by the site. As with any website you visit, each registry sees your connection's IP address and applies its own privacy policy.

Currency conversion

The currency converter also sends a request, only when you click “Get the Bank of Canada rate”: your browser asks the Bank of Canada's Valet service directly for the rate of the chosen currency around the chosen date, in the same way. The amount you convert is never sent. The Bank of Canada sees your connection's IP address and applies its own privacy policy.

Stored on your device only

To save you time, some fields are remembered in your browser's local storage, on your device only. You can erase them at any time with the “Clear everything” button of the invoice generator, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If this changes, this page will be updated and your consent will be requested where the law requires it.

Hosting

Like any web host, GitHub Pages may log technical connection data (such as IP addresses) for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
