The self-employed tax year in Canada

  • April 30: pay your balance owing for the previous year, including CPP contributions on your self-employment income.
  • June 15: file your T1 return with Form T2125 (and your TP-1 in Quebec).
  • Instalments on the 15th of March, June, September and December, if you owe enough tax.
  • GST/HST once you're registered: check the right rate for your province with the GST/HST calculator.

The CRA considers a payment on time if it is received on the next business day when a due date falls on a weekend or holiday. For information only.
