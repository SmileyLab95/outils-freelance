Good to know

These details are for your client's tax credits

According to Revenu Québec, the GST/HST and QST rules impose no special invoice format, except for restaurants and taxis in Quebec. The list follows the information a client registered for GST/HST needs to claim input tax credits for the tax you charge (and, in Quebec, input tax refunds for the QST). The higher the invoice total, the more details it needs: the thresholds are $100 and $500, taxes included. A client who isn't registered, such as a consumer, claims no tax credit.
