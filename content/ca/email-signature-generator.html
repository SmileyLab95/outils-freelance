What CASL requires

    Email signatures and Canada's anti-spam legislation

    • Commercial electronic message: an email that has as its purpose, or one of its purposes, to encourage participation in a commercial activity, including offering to sell a service, advertising, or promoting a person as someone who does so. The Act looks at the content, the links and the contact information in the message.
    • Identification: the name you carry on business under (or your name if you do not use another), your mailing address, and a phone number, an email address or a website, set out clearly and prominently.
    • Unsubscribe: a free way to unsubscribe by the same electronic means, with an email address or web link to send the request to, valid for 60 days. Requests must take effect within 10 business days.
    • Consent: commercial emails also need the recipient's consent, express or implied. Emails that only send a quote the person asked for, facilitate, complete or confirm a transaction they agreed to, or deliver a service they are entitled to are exempt from consent, but not from identification and unsubscribe: the generator includes both in every signature.
