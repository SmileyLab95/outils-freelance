Before you incorporate

A corporation is a separate legal entity, with its own tax return and its own accounts. Check that it's worth it with the sole proprietor vs corporation calculator, and plan the transfer of your business with an accountant.

Good to know

  • Insurance: the business insurance checker covers workers' compensation once you pay employees.
  • Invoices: use the corporation's name and registration numbers from now on; see the invoice checklist.
