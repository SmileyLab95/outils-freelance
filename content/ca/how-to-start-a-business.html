Sole proprietor: what to know before you start

As a sole proprietor, your business isn't separate from you: you report its income on your own return, with your CPP contributions (QPP in Quebec) on your net business income. Registration rules also depend on your province or territory, so check its website too.

Before you start, compare with a corporation in the sole proprietor vs corporation comparison, set your rate with the hourly rate calculator and see how much to set aside for taxes from each payment.
