Sole proprietor: what to know before you start

As a sole proprietor, your business isn't separate from you: its profit goes on your own return (Schedule C), and you pay self-employment tax for Social Security and Medicare (Schedule SE) on top of income tax. Rules also depend on your state and city, so check their websites too.

Before you start, compare with an S corporation in the S corp comparison, set your rate with the hourly rate calculator and see how much to set aside for taxes from each payment.
