How a fixed-fee price is built

A fixed fee is agreed up front, whatever time the work actually takes. It starts from your time estimate for each task, plus a contingency buffer for revisions, meetings and waiting for approvals. That time, multiplied by your rate, gives your fees; the expenses you include in the price (travel, purchases) are added on top.

Write in the estimate what the price includes (deliverables, number of revision rounds) and what will be billed on top: that's what protects a fixed fee from scope creep. The invoice and estimate generator turns it into a document, and the rate converter shows your rate per day, month or year.
