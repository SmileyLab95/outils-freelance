How the minimum is calculated

The IRS does not penalize you if, by each due date, your withholding and estimated payments add up to a quarter of the required annual payment. That amount is the smaller of:

  • 90% of the tax you will owe for 2026, or
  • 100% of the total tax on your 2025 return (110% if your 2025 adjusted gross income was over $150,000), provided you filed a 2025 return covering 12 months.

Paying the prior-year amount is the safest choice when your income grows: you know the number in advance and avoid penalties, even if you owe more when you file. If your income drops, 90% of this year's tax may be lower.
