What your emails must include

    Email signatures and CAN-SPAM

    • Client emails: an email whose primary purpose is to facilitate, complete or confirm a transaction your client agreed to, or to deliver the work they are entitled to, is a transactional or relationship message. A link to your website in the signature does not, by itself, make it a commercial email.
    • Marketing emails: an email whose primary purpose is to advertise or promote your services is a commercial email. It must include a valid physical postal address and a clear way to opt out that keeps working for at least 30 days, and, unless the person agreed to receive it, say clearly that it is an advertisement or solicitation.
