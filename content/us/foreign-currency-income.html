The rule: the rate on the day you receive it

Everything on your US return is in US dollars. If your functional currency is the US dollar, as for a US sole proprietor, translate each item you receive, pay or accrue in a foreign currency at the exchange rate prevailing at that time. The IRS has no official exchange rate: it generally accepts any posted rate that is used consistently, from your bank or from the government sources the IRS lists, such as the Treasury Department and the Federal Reserve.

Foreign clients come with other questions (Form 1099, foreign tax withheld, FBAR): see invoicing international clients.

Why there's no “get the rate” button

The tools on this site run in your browser and only call official services that allow it. We haven't yet found a US government source of daily rates that a browser can read directly, so you enter the rate yourself; nothing you type is sent anywhere (privacy).
