      Good habits with a business account

      • All business money in, all business expenses out: deposit every business receipt and pay business expenses from it.
      • Paying yourself: the IRS suggests writing checks to yourself only when you take money out of the business for personal use.
      • Reconcile every month with your records: it makes your quarterly estimated taxes much easier.
