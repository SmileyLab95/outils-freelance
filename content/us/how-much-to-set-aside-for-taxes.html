A simple system that works

  1. Open a separate savings account just for taxes.
  2. Every time a client pays you, move your percentage to it the same day. Don't wait for the end of the month.
  3. Pay your quarterly estimated taxes from that account: the quarterly tax calculator gives the minimum to avoid a penalty, and the tax calendar reminds you of each due date.
  4. Recalculate every few months as your income changes: the percentage rises with your profit.

Anything left in the account after you file in April is a bonus, or a head start on next year.
