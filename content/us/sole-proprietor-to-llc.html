Before you switch

By default, the IRS treats a single-member LLC like a sole proprietorship: its income still goes on your Schedule C, so forming one mainly changes your liability protection. Electing S corporation status changes your federal taxes: see whether it's worth it with the sole proprietor vs S corp calculator, which counts payroll and filing costs.
