Standard mileage rate or actual expenses?

With the standard rate you multiply your business miles by the IRS rate, which covers gas, maintenance, insurance and depreciation. With the actual expense method you deduct the business share of all your car costs. The standard rate is simpler and often better for fuel-efficient cars; actual expenses can be higher for expensive vehicles driven mostly for business.
