Invoice due dates in the US

  • Net terms: Net 30 means 30 days after the invoice date; Net 30 EOM means 30 days after the end of the month of the invoice.
  • No date in the contract: the New York City, New York State and Illinois laws require payment no later than 30 days after you complete the work. Contract value thresholds and exclusions apply: the Freelance Isn't Free guide covers them, along with other states and cities.
  • Always in writing: put the due date in your contract and on every invoice, with any late fee you agreed.
