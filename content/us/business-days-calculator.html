Business days, federal holidays and deadlines

  • Business days: the days you work, Monday to Friday by default, minus the federal holidays you take off.
  • Federal holidays: the 11 holidays set by federal law (5 U.S.C. 6103), on the dates the U.S. Office of Personnel Management lists for most federal employees. A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, one that falls on a Sunday on the Monday after: the calculator marks these days “observed.”
  • Calendar days: every day, weekends and federal holidays included.

Your billable days

To set your rate, start from the business days in the year, take off your vacation and the time you can't bill (finding clients, admin, learning): the hourly rate calculator does it for you.

What the calculator does not count

Federal holidays apply to federal employees. Your state, your clients and your bank may close on other days, or stay open on some of these: check their calendars. A deadline set by a contract or by law is counted according to its own rules: check them for any deadline that affects your rights.

OPM publishes federal holidays several years ahead, currently up to 2030; we add each new year when it is published. For information only.
