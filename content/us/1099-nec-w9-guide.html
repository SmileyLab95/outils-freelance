If you're the freelancer: W-9 and the 1099s you receive

Filling in a W-9

  • Line 1: your legal name as shown on your tax return. Line 2: your business or LLC name, if different.
  • Tax classification: “Individual/sole proprietor” for most freelancers, including single-member LLCs that haven't elected to be taxed as a corporation.
  • Taxpayer ID: your Social Security number or an EIN. An EIN is free from the IRS and keeps your SSN off client paperwork.
  • Send it securely to the client. It never goes to the IRS.

The 1099s you'll receive

  • 1099-NEC: from each client that paid you $2,000 or more by check or bank transfer in 2026.
  • 1099-K: from payment platforms (PayPal, Stripe, marketplaces) when you received more than $20,000 over more than 200 transactions.
  • Report all your income on Schedule C, whether or not it appears on a 1099. Estimate what you'll owe with the self-employment tax calculator and set the right share aside with how much to set aside.
