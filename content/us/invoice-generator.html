How to get paid faster

  • Invoice right away, the day the work is delivered or the milestone is reached.
  • Use short terms: Net 15 or due on receipt for small clients, Net 30 at most for companies.
  • Make paying easy: include a payment link or your ACH details on every invoice.
  • Ask for a deposit on estimates (30 to 50%) before you start.
  • State your late fee in your contract and on the invoice, and follow up with our payment reminder generator.

New client asking for your Social Security number? Send a completed Form W-9 instead. Clients who pay you $2,000 or more in 2026 may send you a Form 1099-NEC early in 2027.
