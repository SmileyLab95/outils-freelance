Making the most of pension relief

  • Pay in before 5 April for the relief to count in the current tax year. The tax calendar lists the key dates.
  • Higher-rate taxpayer? Your extra relief reduces the balancing payment and your payments on account: see the effect with the self-employed tax calculator.
  • Running a limited company? Employer contributions from the company also save Corporation Tax and National Insurance: compare with the sole trader vs limited company calculator.
