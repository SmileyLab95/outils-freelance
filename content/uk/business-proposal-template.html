What a winning proposal covers

  • The client's situation, in their words: show that you understood the need before talking about yourself.
  • What you propose: the approach and the result, not a list of technologies.
  • Steps, timing and fees: a clear table, a total, the deposit and the payment terms.
  • Optional extras: they let the client adjust the budget without reopening the whole project.
  • Next steps and a validity date: the client knows what to do and by when.
