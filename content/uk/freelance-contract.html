What every freelance contract should cover

  • Scope and deliverables: the more precise, the fewer arguments about what's included.
  • Fees, VAT, deposit and payment terms: a deposit protects your cash flow, and the Late Payment Act gives you interest and compensation. Chase late invoices with a payment reminder letter.
  • Status: a contract for services, with control and substitution, reflects that you're self-employed.
  • Ownership: assigning the IP only once you're paid in full is your best protection against non-payment.
  • Termination: notice, and payment for the work already done.
