How statutory interest works

Interest = amount × (8% + reference rate) × days late ÷ 365

The reference rate is the Bank of England base rate on 31 December (for debts that became overdue between 1 January and 30 June) or on 30 June (for debts overdue between 1 July and 31 December). It stays fixed for that debt, even if the base rate changes later.

  • Debts overdue in the first half of 2025: 4.75% + 8% = 12.75%.
  • Debts overdue in the second half of 2025: 4.25% + 8% = 12.25%.
  • Debts overdue in 2026: 3.75% + 8% = 11.75%.

Interest runs until the debt is paid. Claiming it is your right, but many freelancers use it as leverage: mention it in your reminder and waive it if the client pays promptly.

Information only, not legal advice. The Act applies to contracts between businesses for the supply of goods or services.
