How the conversion works

An hourly rate is the day rate divided by the hours you bill in a day. A week is 5 billed days. The annual figure is the day rate times the days you bill in a year, and a month is a twelfth of it: an average, since some months have more holiday or gaps between contracts than others.

The number of billed days is what turns a day rate into a yearly turnover. After 5 weeks of holiday and 8 bank holidays, a year has about 227 weekdays left, but part of that time goes to finding work, admin and training, which you can't bill. Enter your own estimate.
