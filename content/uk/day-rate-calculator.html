The maths

Day rate = (profit needed + expenses) ÷ billable days × (1 + safety margin)

The profit needed is found exactly with 2026/27 income tax and Class 4 National Insurance, so the rate stays right in every tax band, including the tapering of the Personal Allowance above £100,000.

Billable days start from 260 weekdays, minus your holidays and bank holidays, minus the share of time you cannot bill.
