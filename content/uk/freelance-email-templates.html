Freelance email templates

The emails every freelancer has to write, ready to use. Copy one, fill in the highlighted bits, send.

1. Cold outreach to a prospect
Subject: A quick idea for [Company] Hi [First name], I noticed [specific detail: a recent launch, your website, a job advert] and it made me think of [one concrete improvement]. I help [type of clients] [measurable result]. For example, [similar client] saw [number] within [time frame]. Would a 15-minute call next week be useful? I'd be happy to share two or three specific ideas, with no obligation. Best wishes, [Your name]
2. Chasing a quote
Subject: Re: Quote for [project name] Hi [First name], Have you had a chance to look at the quote? If anything doesn't quite work (scope, timings or budget), just let me know: it's often easy to adjust. To start on [date], I'd need your go-ahead by [date]. Kind regards, [Your name]
3. Announcing a rate rise
Subject: My rates from [date] Hi [First name], Thank you for another great year working together. From [date, at least a month away], my day rate for new work will be [new rate]. This reflects [more experience / additional services / rising costs] and lets me keep giving your projects the attention they deserve. Anything booked before [date] stays at the current rate. Happy to talk it through if you have any questions. Best wishes, [Your name]
4. Handling scope creep
Subject: Re: [request] Hi [First name], Great idea, I'd be glad to help with [new request]. It sits outside the scope we agreed, so here are two options: 1. I add it as extra work for [price], delivered by [date]. 2. We swap it for [an item still in scope] at no extra cost. Which would you prefer? Kind regards, [Your name]
5. “Your price is too high”
Hi [First name], Thanks for being open about it. Just so I understand: is the total over your budget, or is the value of some parts unclear? If it's the budget, we can reduce the scope to fit [their budget], for example by [removing or phasing an item]. I'd rather adjust what's included than cut corners on quality. What would work best for you? [Your name]
6. Asking for a testimonial
Subject: A small favour? Hi [First name], I really enjoyed working with you on [project]. Would you be happy to write two or three sentences about the experience? It helps other clients decide to work with me. A few prompts if useful: what was the challenge, what changed afterwards, and would you recommend me? And if you know anyone who could use [your service], I'd be grateful for an introduction. Many thanks, [Your name]
