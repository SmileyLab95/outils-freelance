When a UK invoice becomes late

  • Agreed payment date: the invoice is due on the date you agreed, for example 30 days after the invoice. Statutory interest starts the day after.
  • No agreed date: the due date is the last day of a period of 30 days beginning with the day you provided the service or, if later, the day your client had notice of the amount (usually when they received the invoice).
  • More than 60 days: with a business client, if the agreed date falls after the end of a 60-day period, the end of that period applies, unless the agreed date is not grossly unfair to you.
  • Public authorities: the agreed date cannot fall after the end of the 30-day period.

After the due date

Unless you agreed a different, substantial remedy in your contract, you can claim statutory interest and a fixed sum for recovery costs: the late payment interest calculator works them out, and a payment reminder letter helps you ask for payment.

Advance payments and contracts with an acceptance or verification procedure have their own rules, not covered here. For information only: this tool is not legal advice.
