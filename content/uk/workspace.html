Everything a sole trader needs

  • Quotes and invoices laid out like the invoice generator: VAT only if you're registered, PO number, numbers in sequence (2026-001, 2026-002…), PDF and a prefilled email.
  • Payments: partial payments, overdue invoices flagged, a reminder email ready to send, with the statutory interest you can claim from a business client; credit notes to cancel or correct an invoice.
  • Income on the cash basis, by tax year (6 April to 5 April) and by quarter, refunds deducted, export to CSV.
  • Expenses sorted by box of the self-employment page, with HMRC's list of what is and isn't allowable for each box; export to CSV.
  • Self-employment summary for the tax year, box by box (SA103S), with the trading income allowance if it suits you better: a preparation aid, not a tax return.
  • Tax: your estimated income tax and Class 4, worked out like the self-employed tax calculator, your payments on account and balancing payment, and the VAT registration threshold over the last 12 months.
  • Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: whether it applies to you and from when, then your quarterly update periods, deadlines and totals by category.
  • Time tracking: a timer or quick entries in hours or days, billable time by client, and an invoice drafted from your time in one click.
  • Leads: your pipeline by stage, today's follow-ups, a do-not-contact list, and the PECR rules for marketing by email, text and phone.
  • Cash flow and deadlines: tax already owed, your projected balance over 3, 6 or 12 months, late clients, and the UK tax calendar for your situation.

Not in this version: traditional (accruals) accounting, VAT returns, capital allowances, losses, property income, partnerships and limited companies, student loan repayments, and sending anything to HMRC.

For a one-off invoice without saving anything, the invoice generator is still there. To see every deadline and add it to your calendar, open the UK tax calendar.

Tax amounts are estimates; you file and pay with HMRC. For information only, not tax advice.
