Working days, bank holidays and deadlines

  • Working days: the days you work, Monday to Friday by default, minus the bank holidays you take off.
  • Bank holidays: the official list published by GOV.UK for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, including substitute days and one-off bank holidays.
  • Calendar days: every day, weekends and bank holidays included.

Your billable days

To set your rate, start from the working days in the year, take off your holidays and the time you can't bill (finding work, admin, training): the day rate calculator does it for you.

What the calculator does not count

Local holidays and days your clients close are not included. A deadline set by a contract or by law is counted according to its own rules: check them for any deadline that affects your rights.

GOV.UK publishes bank holidays a few years ahead, currently up to 2028; we add each new year when it is published. For information only.
