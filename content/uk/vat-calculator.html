    VAT for freelancers and sole traders

    • Registration threshold: £90,000 of VAT taxable turnover over any rolling 12 months. Below it, you don't charge VAT unless you register voluntarily.
    • Invoices: once registered, your invoices must show your VAT number, the VAT rate and the VAT amount. A simplified VAT invoice is allowed for supplies up to £250. Our invoice generator adds the VAT line for you.
    • Flat Rate Scheme: for turnover up to £150,000, pay HMRC a fixed percentage of your gross turnover; the limited cost trader rate is 16.5%.

    Information only. Check the VAT treatment of your goods or services on GOV.UK.
