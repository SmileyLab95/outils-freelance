      How sole traders are taxed

      As a sole trader you pay income tax and Class 4 National Insurance on your profit: what you earn minus allowable business expenses. You report it once a year through Self Assessment, for the tax year running from 6 April to 5 April.

      Scotland sets its own income tax bands: 19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45% and 48% in 2026/27. National Insurance is the same across the UK.

      Not included

      • Student loan repayments, which are collected through Self Assessment on top of tax.
      • Savings, dividend and rental income, and pension contributions, which can change your bands.
      • Marriage allowance, Blind Person's Allowance and other reliefs.
