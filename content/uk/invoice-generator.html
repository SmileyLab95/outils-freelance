Getting paid faster in the UK

  • Send the invoice the day you finish, with your bank details and a clear due date.
  • Use short terms such as 14 days, and agree them in writing before you start.
  • Ask for a deposit on quotes for larger projects.
  • Know your rights: between businesses, you can claim statutory interest and £40 to £100 compensation on late invoices. Use the late payment interest calculator and the reminder letters.
