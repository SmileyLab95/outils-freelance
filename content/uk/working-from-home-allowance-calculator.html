HMRC flat rates for working from home

Hours at home in the monthFlat rate
25 to 50£10
51 to 100£18
101 or more£26

The flat rate covers heating, lighting and power. Claim the business share of your phone and broadband on top. If you prefer actual costs, split your bills in a way you can explain, and keep your records. The flat rate is for sole traders and partnerships without a company as a partner.

Deduct your claim from your profit, then see the effect with the self-employed tax calculator; for travel, use the mileage allowance calculator.
