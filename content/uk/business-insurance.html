    What the law requires

    InsuranceWho needs it
    Employers' liabilityAny employer, from the first employee: at least £5 million of cover from an authorised insurer. Not needed if your only employees are close family members (except in a limited company) or are based outside England, Scotland and Wales.
    GOV.UK, HSE
    Motor insuranceAny vehicle used in your business, a personal car included.
    Business.gov.uk

    Worth having

    • Public liability: needed by almost all businesses, especially if you deal with the public or if a contract requires it.
    • Professional indemnity: if you give advice or professional services; compulsory if your regulator or your industry requires it.

    Compare quotes from at least two insurers and read the exclusions and limits before you sign.
