    What a UK business email signature must show

    • Limited company: its registered name on all business correspondence, and on business letters its company number, registered office address and the part of the UK where it is registered. The 2015 regulations treat a document in electronic form as a document, so emails count. The name usually shows it is a limited company with "Limited" or "Ltd"; the generator says so if your name does not.
    • Sole trader with a business name: your own name and a UK address where documents can be served on you, on business letters, written orders, invoices, receipts and demands for payment.
    • Sole trader under your own name: no particular details are required.

    Directors and share capital

    If a business letter names a director other than in the text or as the signatory, it must name every director. Showing your share capital is optional; if you show it, it must be the paid-up capital.
